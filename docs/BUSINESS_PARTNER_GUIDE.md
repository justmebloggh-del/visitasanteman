# Business partner guide

## Getting listed
1. Go to **/partner** → *Register your business*. Choose your business type (accommodation provider, restaurant owner, tour operator, boat operator, tour guide, or general).
2. Verify your email using the link we send, log in, and the business account is activated at **/partner/activate** (pre-filled from registration).
3. **My listings → New listing**. Fill in your details, add a main photo and gallery photos, then **Save draft** or **Submit for review**.
4. Our team reviews every listing. Approved listings go live; you can then see page views and enquiries.

## Things to know
- You can only see and edit **your own** listings.
- You cannot publish, verify, feature or change your plan yourself. Those are done by an administrator.
- **Editing a live listing sends it back to "pending review"** and hides it until approved. This keeps unreviewed content off the public site.
- Rooms (accommodation) and menu items (restaurants) are managed on the listing page and are not reviewed separately. Only add prices you can honour.
- Gallery size depends on your plan (Free 3, Standard 10, Premium 30 by default; admins can change these).
- **Enquiries** are requests from visitors, not confirmed bookings. Reply by email or WhatsApp. Online booking and payment come in a later phase.
- **Bookings:** visitors send booking requests (stays, tables, experiences). Open **Bookings**, check availability, then **Confirm** with a price. The customer is emailed. Nothing is booked until you confirm. If online payment is switched on you can **Request online payment**; otherwise arrange payment directly. Mark **completed** after the stay so the guest can leave a review.
- **Availability:** on a listing, block dates per room. Confirmed bookings automatically use up a room's units.
- **Reviews:** only guests with a completed booking can review; our team approves them first. You can reply publicly.
- **Subscription:** ask about a paid plan from the Subscription page. An administrator activates it; nothing is charged online yet.

## For administrators
- Review: *Admin → Accommodation / Restaurants / Businesses*, filter **Pending review**, open, check details with the owner, tick **Verified badge** only after verifying, then **Publish**.
- Plans: *Admin → Subscription plans* edits names, prices (blank = "contact us"), photo allowance, benefits, featured placement and analytics flags.
- Assign a plan on the listing form (*Subscription plan*). Tick **Featured on homepage** to give featured placement.
- Partner enquiries to you (plan requests) arrive in *Enquiries* as "partnership".
