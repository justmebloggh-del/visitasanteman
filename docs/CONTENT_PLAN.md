# Content collection plan (regional, district by district)

The site publishes only verified information. Content is the main launch blocker, and the region is much larger than one lake, so work district by district rather than everywhere at once.

## Approach
1. Load the districts from an official list (see the deployment guide). Pick **3 to 5 pilot districts** that have the most visitor interest and the most willing local contacts. Reach credible depth there before widening.
2. For each district, keep a simple tracker: contacts, what has been collected, who verified it, and when (`last_verified_at` and `verification_notes` in the admin).
3. Publish a district's places only when they are verified. Empty districts still show a clear "being prepared" notice and stay out of the sitemap.

## Who to involve (per district)
The district, municipal or metropolitan assembly (tourism and planning officers), traditional authorities, cultural and heritage custodians, community leaders, hotel and restaurant owners, tour operators and guides, local photographers, and the Ghana Tourism Authority regional office.

## Per attraction (admin → Attractions)
| Field | How to get it | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| District | From the official list; the assembly can confirm boundaries | Regional editor |
| GPS point | Phone GPS on site, both lat and lng | Second visit or official map |
| Opening hours, fees | Ask the operator, in writing or WhatsApp | Operator |
| History / customs | Written source or elder interview; record who | District assembly or traditional authority |
| Photos | Commission or written permission, with credit | Photographer |
| Accessibility, guidelines | On-site check | Staff |
Fill **Sources**; a regional editor ticks **Content verified** and sets **Last verified on** only when done.

## Per community
Introduction, verified history, festivals, traditional food, directions, GPS, photos, and the district it belongs to. No population figures unless sourced. Ten communities around Lake Bosomtwe exist as empty drafts (Abono, Abrodwum, Amakom, Ankaase, Pipie, Adwafo, Kuntanase, Esereso, Brodekwano, Old Brodekwano); the rest of the region's communities are added as each district is worked on.

## Per business / experience
Owner confirms name, phone or WhatsApp, prices, hours, photos. Experience: provider confirms availability before ticking *Provider has confirmed*. Administrators tick *Verified* on a listing only after a call or visit.

## Culture and heritage
Asante history, traditional institutions, festivals, Adinkra and other symbols are **not written by the development team**. Draft each piece from cited sources or interviews, have the appropriate custodians review it, record who reviewed it in *Verification notes*, then a regional editor publishes. Lake Bosomtwe has customary rules and sacred significance; the destination page and visitor guidelines need traditional-authority review before publishing.

## Minimum for a credible launch
3 to 5 pilot districts, each with several attractions (photos and coordinates), a few stays and places to eat; the pilot communities published; About, Our mission, Explore, Travel Guide, Privacy and Terms written; contact details set. Other districts can show "being prepared" honestly.

## Pages to write (admin → Pages)
About, Our mission, Partners, Explore, Travel Guide (getting here and around, best time to visit, safety, etiquette, FAQ), Culture & Heritage and its five sub-pages, Privacy and Terms (legal review).
