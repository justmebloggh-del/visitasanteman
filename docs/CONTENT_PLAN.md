# Content collection plan

The site publishes only verified information. Run this alongside deployment; it is the main launch blocker.

## Who to involve
Bosomtwe District Assembly (tourism/planning officers), traditional authorities of each community, the lake's boat and fishing associations, hotel/restaurant owners, a local photographer.

## Per attraction (admin → Attractions)
| Field | How to get it | Verified by |
|---|---|---|
| GPS point | Phone GPS on site, both lat and lng | Second visit or official map |
| Opening hours, fees | Ask the operator, in writing/WhatsApp | Operator |
| History / customs | Written source or elder interview; record who | District Assembly or traditional authority |
| Photos | Commission or written permission, with credit | Photographer |
| Accessibility, guidelines | On-site check | Staff |
Fill the **Sources** field and tick **Content verified** only when done.

## Per community (10 seeded as drafts)
Abono, Abrodwum, Amakom, Ankaase, Pipie, Adwafo, Kuntanase, Esereso, Brodekwano, Old Brodekwano: introduction, verified history, festivals, traditional food, directions, GPS, photos. No population figures unless sourced.

## Per business / experience
Owner confirms name, phone/WhatsApp, prices, hours, photos. Experience: provider confirms availability before ticking *Provider has confirmed*. Tick *Verified* on a listing only after a call or visit.

## Cultural sensitivity
The lake has customary rules and sacred significance. Have traditional authorities review the Explore page, visitor guidelines and any ritual or taboo wording before publishing.

## Minimum for a credible launch
5+ attractions with photos and coordinates, all communities published, 3+ stays, 3+ places to eat, About/Explore/Travel Guide/Privacy/Terms written, contact details set.

## Pages to write (admin → Pages)
About, Explore (overview, location, natural history from cited sources), Travel Guide (route from Kumasi, transport, best time to visit, safety, etiquette, FAQ), Privacy, Terms (legal review).
