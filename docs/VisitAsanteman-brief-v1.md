# VISITASANTEMAN
## Complete Rebranding and Digital Platform Architecture

**Project:** VisitAsanteman  
**Former identity:** Visit Lake Bosomtwe / Visit Bosomtwe  
**New geographical scope:** The entire Ashanti Region, Ghana  
**Project type:** Regional tourism promotion, destination discovery, tourism business directory, travel planning and destination management platform  
**Primary tagline:** Discover Ashanti. Experience Our Heritage.

---

# 1. Executive vision

VisitAsanteman will be a comprehensive digital tourism platform dedicated to promoting the tourism assets, cultural heritage, natural attractions, hospitality industry, events, communities and investment opportunities of the Ashanti Region of Ghana.

It will serve as a central online destination where local and international visitors can discover what the region offers, explore places to visit, find accommodation, locate restaurants, book experiences, learn about Asante history and culture, plan itineraries and connect with local tourism businesses.

The platform must not be designed as a conventional tourism blog or a simple directory. It should evolve into a regional tourism ecosystem that supports visitors, communities, tourism operators, hospitality businesses, cultural institutions, investors and public-sector stakeholders.

The long-term objective is to make VisitAsanteman a recognised digital gateway to tourism in the Ashanti Region.

## 1.1 Strategic objectives

1. Promote the Ashanti Region as a diverse tourism destination.
2. Increase awareness of established and lesser-known attractions.
3. Promote Asante history, traditions, festivals, architecture, crafts, food and cultural experiences.
4. Connect tourists with hotels, guesthouses, restaurants, tour operators and other tourism businesses.
5. Support community-based tourism and local entrepreneurship.
6. Improve the visibility of tourism opportunities across the region.
7. Help visitors discover destinations and plan practical travel itineraries.
8. Create opportunities for tourism businesses to advertise and generate enquiries or bookings.
9. Attract responsible private-sector investment and strategic partnerships.
10. Support sustainable tourism, environmental conservation and local economic development.

## 1.2 Target audiences

- Ghanaian domestic tourists.
- International tourists.
- African diaspora and visitors interested in Asante heritage.
- Families, couples and group travellers.
- Educational institutions and cultural researchers.
- Adventure, nature and ecotourism visitors.
- Tour operators and travel agencies.
- Hotels, resorts, guesthouses and restaurants.
- Event organisers and cultural institutions.
- Local entrepreneurs and artisans.
- Tourism investors, development organisations and government institutions.

# 3. Geographical architecture

This is one of the most important changes.

The platform must cover the **entire Ashanti Region**, rather than treating Lake Bosomtwe as its main geographical boundary.

## 3.1 Regional structure

Use the following geographical hierarchy:

**Ghana → Ashanti Region → Metropolitan/Municipal/District Assembly area → Community/Settlement → Tourism location or business.**

The database must support the region's administrative geography without hardcoding a fixed list into the application.

Create an administrative geography management module so authorised administrators can add, edit, deactivate and reorganise geographical records when official administrative boundaries or names change.

Recommended entities:

- Region.
- Metropolitan, Municipal or District Assembly (MMDA).
- Traditional area, where verified and relevant.
- Community or settlement.
- Locality or neighbourhood, where needed.
- Tourism attraction.
- Tourism business.
- Event venue.

## 3.2 Geographical coverage

The initial coverage should include all metropolitan, municipal and district areas within the Ashanti Region as verified against current official government records.

Do not assume that a list of towns or popular tourist destinations constitutes complete regional coverage.

The platform must allow administrators to maintain a verified list of MMDAs and assign each tourism listing to its correct geographical area.

## 3.3 Regional destination discovery

Visitors should be able to explore the region by:

- MMDA.
- Town or community.
- Attraction category.
- Tourism experience.
- Distance from a selected location.
- Popularity or editorial recommendation.
- Accessibility and visitor suitability.

Each MMDA should have a dedicated destination landing page featuring:

- Overview and introduction.
- Location and map.
- Attractions.
- Cultural and heritage sites.
- Accommodation.
- Restaurants and food experiences.
- Events and festivals.
- Tour operators and activities.
- Local businesses.
- Photo and video gallery.
- Travel information.
- Related articles.

## 3.4 Geographical data quality

Every published location should have:

- Verified name and spelling.
- Correct administrative area.
- Location description.
- Map coordinates where available.
- Source or verification notes.
- Content status.
- Last verification date.

Never invent coordinates, administrative affiliations, traditional ownership or historical claims.

# 20. Project operating principles

The following rules must guide all project decisions.

**Accuracy first:** Never fabricate attractions, history, prices, contact details, opening hours, coordinates, statistics or investment opportunities.

**Regional inclusion:** Do not allow the platform to become Kumasi-only or Lake Bosomtwe-only. Promote destinations and tourism businesses throughout the Ashanti Region.

**Community benefit:** Make it easy for smaller communities, local guides, artisans and tourism entrepreneurs to gain visibility.

**Cultural respect:** Treat Asante history, traditional institutions, symbols and customs accurately and respectfully.

**Sustainability:** Promote responsible tourism and avoid presenting environmentally harmful or unapproved developments as established attractions.

**Commercial practicality:** Build revenue opportunities that are transparent and beneficial to the platform and its participating businesses.

**Technical quality:** Use secure, maintainable, scalable code and proper database design.

**Functional completeness:** Every visible action must work. Do not use placeholder functionality in production.

**Evidence-based publication:** Use official, credible and traceable sources for factual tourism information. Record verification status and last-checked dates.
